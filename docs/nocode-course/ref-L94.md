## For our version

- **This is 5:05 of slides and it is correct that way.** The exception to the
  footage rule, and the only place in Week 3 where a montage is the right answer.
- **Make the recap a montage of OUR OWN footage, not text.** Every beat he names,
  we already shot: the game, the MVP, the Jira PR, the SaaS demo, the tmux grid,
  the trader workstation. **Two seconds each under the narration.** That turns the
  weakest-looking lecture into the strongest sizzle reel in the program — and it
  is nearly free, because the footage exists.
- **The board completing is the closing frame of the entire course.** Build it as a
  proper animated payoff: the three strips filling one after another. **This is
  the one graphic in Day 5 worth real time.**
- **Six projects, not four** — keep the joke and count them on screen. It reframes
  the whole program as more substantial than it felt.
- **Adapt the personal close, don't copy it.** *"You get me forever"* is his voice
  and his channel. **Ours needs its own sign-off** and its own call to action —
  where to post, where to reach us. **NEEDED FROM YOU: how you want students to
  reach you** (LinkedIn, a channel, an email alias) and whether we ask for ratings.
- **Substitution:** his recap names Codex twice. Ours names **Cursor**, consistent
  with L91–L93, and names whichever tool our own parade crowned.
- **"Go build" is the last thing said.** Do not add anything after it — no outro
  card, no next-course pitch stacked on top. Let it end.
- **Footage: ~60%** as a montage, 0% as live demo.
