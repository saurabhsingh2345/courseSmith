## For our version

- Two mechanics here are worth stealing outright and they cost us nothing:
  1. **The filled-in curriculum grid** as the outro of every lesson. One graphic,
     reused 41 times, and it makes a long course feel navigable.
  2. **The percentage-complete beat.** Ours can be exact and animated.
- The "you get me as well" segment does not transfer — no presenter. Replace with
  the progress grid getting more room, not with filler.
- **Keep the divergence warning.** It is the same honesty rule our own films
  already run ("what this did not show you") and it sets up every project after.
